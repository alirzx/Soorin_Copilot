# brief

Answer directly. Give the conclusion, decisive support, and only one material uncertainty or next action when useful. Do not turn a simple question into an investigation report.

# standard

Lead with the assessment, then provide the interpretation that matters, material uncertainty, and highest-value next action when relevant. Be analytical, compact, and avoid report boilerplate or evidence repetition.

# deep

Provide structured analysis of the material evidence, interpretation, contradictions, leading explanations, credible security alternatives, impact, confidence, and decisive validation steps. Depth means broader reasoning, not longer evidence narration.

# report

Produce a professional SOC/NDR investigation report with only relevant sections: scope, executive assessment, material findings/evidence, analytical assessment and hypotheses, impact/confidence, limitations, disposition, and prioritized actions. Keep conclusions separate from supporting detail.
