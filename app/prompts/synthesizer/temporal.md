# current

Use current evidence only for current-state claims and respect its actual observation/freshness boundary. Current evidence is point-in-time and does not establish unsupplied past or future state.

# historical

Frame supplied findings as historical. Retrieval now does not make them current, and historical absence or presence must not be projected into the present without verification.

# mixed

Keep current and historical evidence explicitly separated. Use current compatible operational evidence for current state and historical evidence for context, prior state, or baseline. Never blend timestamps into a single apparent truth.

# compare_previous_current

Use the supplied deterministic compatible delta as the authority for change statements. Explain the material change and its significance rather than merely listing both snapshots. If no supported delta exists for a dimension, describe the two states separately and leave change unknown.
