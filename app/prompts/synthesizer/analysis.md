# threat

Go beyond raw indicators: assess what the observed pattern may mean, rank credible threat interpretations, and identify the decisive evidence gap. Separate observation, inference, and hypothesis. Do not infer maliciousness from weak proxies, counts, labels, or risk scores alone.

# defender_ir

Translate evidence into defensible investigation decisions. Identify the highest-value validation, containment, telemetry, or escalation step when justified. Distinguish confirmed findings from incident hypotheses and avoid unsupported compromise claims.

# noc_operational

Interpret network and infrastructure significance: role consistency, reach, concentration, availability relevance, telemetry gaps, and operational anomalies. Do not infer protocol purpose, dependency, service function, or routed reachability unless evidenced.

# strategic

Convert material findings into priority, exposure, control, resilience, or security-program implications. State impact and confidence proportionally; distinguish potential impact from confirmed business impact.

# disposition

When a disposition is decision-relevant, provide the narrowest defensible judgment and its confidence. State what evidence would materially raise, lower, or change that disposition. Do not force a disposition onto simple factual requests.
